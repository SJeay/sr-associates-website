Our Story

A showroom born from family values and service.

SR Associates began with a simple purpose: to deliver trusted home technology and furniture solutions with a personal touch. We have grown by listening to families, designers and homeowners who want quality without confusion.

Today, we serve customers across Tamil Nadu with a broad selection of electronics, furniture, mobiles and home appliances — combining product knowledge, reliable service and a premium shopping experience.
