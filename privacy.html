Privacy Policy

How this website handles information when you browse or contact our showroom.

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This policy describes the SR Associates website and its current enquiry options. Our showroom locations are Pollachi Main Road, Meenakshipuram, Pollachi, Tamil Nadu 642103, and Opp. MRC Mill, 2/43-B, Sethumadai Road, Annamalai, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu 642104.

Information you enter on this site

The enquiry form on our contact page currently checks the fields in your browser only. It is not connected to a submission service, and information entered there is not sent to or stored by SR Associates through this website. Please contact us by phone or WhatsApp if you would like a response.

Phone and WhatsApp enquiries

If you choose to call or open WhatsApp, you share information directly with SR Associates through the relevant service. WhatsApp is operated by Meta, and its handling of your account and messages is governed by its own privacy information. We use details you choose to share to respond to your enquiry and assist with your visit or purchase.
